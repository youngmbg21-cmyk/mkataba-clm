Decided so far

  • Lifeline is the strip. "Bring up" lands it on the Board below Today's insights, newest first.
  • Duties are called obligations everywhere on the strip: the chart's key, the next-obligation figure, the list and Jambo's count.
  • A long list scrolls inside its own box. The obligations list shows about seven rows, with its headings staying in view. The card keeps the same height whether a contract has six obligations or sixty.
  • History keeps today's tools: search, Show the wording, and the Filter for clause, person, round and outcome. Side (All sides · Ours · Theirs) now sits in the open instead of behind Filter. The trail scrolls inside the card too.
  • Verify integrity, export and print stay in the trail's ⋯ More, as today.

What the strip reads

  • Everything on it comes from the upload. That means the Fill in details (parties, value, dates, renewal, notice, law, payment, liability), the obligations Jambo found and Jambo's first reading (brief, risks, standards). A detail still owed shows in amber, as it does everywhere.
  • The ⋯ menu holds Where we are and History. Each opens inside the same strip, and "‹ Overview" or ⋯ goes back. Pressing ⋯ again closes the menu.
  • The strip's own buttons copy the insight panels: Open (the paper), Keep (stays on your Board), Ask why (Jambo) and Let it go (removes it). × closes it.
  • Contracts on Home (steps 1 to 3) are unchanged.
