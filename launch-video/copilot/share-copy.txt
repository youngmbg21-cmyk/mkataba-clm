HaTi Copilot is the AI inside our Contract Lifecycle Management platform.

It reads every contract you have and:
• writes the brief and flags the watch-outs (auto-renewal, uncapped liability, foreign law),
• turns dense legalese into plain English, clause by clause,
• checks each clause against your playbook and redlines only what's off — using your own precedent,
• answers questions across your whole book: "which contracts auto-renew before year-end?"

And it's honest by design: it shows its work, flags what it's unsure of, and never invents a number. Powered by Claude — your key never leaves your server.
