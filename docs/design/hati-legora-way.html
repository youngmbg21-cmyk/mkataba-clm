A contract's Dossier

Open a contract and its paper stands in the middle, with Jambo's panel beside it. The panel has three tabs: Dossier, Clauses and Redlines. The Dossier is the six stages across the top, then one even list of what to look at and what still waits.

  1. Read the spine: six stages across the top, the current one marked.
  2. Press a line. What it opens comes to the middle, where the paper was; the Dossier never moves. “‹ Paper” or Esc brings the paper back.
  3. Under the list: Obligations, the links, then the stage's two acts at the foot. Every other act is in the paper's More.
  4. On Clauses, each point Jambo found carries its doors as green links. A blank carries only Fill it in.
