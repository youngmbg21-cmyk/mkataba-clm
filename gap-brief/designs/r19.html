Round 19 · Parties and People

This round decides how the two "who" pages look once they land on the desk: Parties (every company we have agreements with) and People (our colleagues and their contacts). Each option draws both; use the small tabs under "‹ The counter", or the S key, to see the other page.

Everything Parties and People do today stays: the NDA column read from confirmed facts, Open party narrowing Contracts, the A–Z of people, ours and theirs. Nothing is built until you pick one by name.

The three ideas

OptionThe ideaWhen it winsWhat it costs
ShelvesEvery party is a card on a wall, its NDA badge in the corner and what waits on you at the foot. People sit on their company's shelf: ours first, then each party's.When you look for a party by recognising it, and want to see who belongs to whom at a glance.About 9 parties fit on a screen instead of 12 or more. Comparing NDAs or money across parties is harder, because the facts are not in columns.
Relationship fileAn index on the left; the one party or person you choose fills the page: facts, what waits on you, their agreements, their people. Jambo's panel says what he sees, from the record, at no cost.When most visits are about one relationship ("what do we have with Nordvik?") rather than the whole list.The list shrinks to names, so "which parties have no NDA?" needs the filter in the index rather than a glance. The file repeats a short list of the party's agreements that Contracts also shows (each row opens the contract; "All 3 on Contracts" is the one door to the full list).
The SAP wayToday's pages on the new desk: the white band with labelled filter boxes, a quiet table with the NDA column, the chosen row's facts in Jambo's panel.When people scan and filter many parties or people at once: "who has no NDA", "who is waiting on me", "who is on Nordvik's side".The least new. Tables look plainer than cards, and on the phone the table keeps only its key columns.

My recommendation: The SAP way

Parties and People are lists you scan and filter, and a table answers the common questions in one look, above all "do we have an NDA with them?", the column you picked the SAP way on 17 October. On the new desk the side panel is simply Jambo's panel, so the chosen party's facts, its NDA sentence and what waits on you sit beside the list without a second panel.

From the Relationship file I would borrow one thing for the panel: the "Waiting on you" row with its button, as drawn in the SAP way's panel too.

The page checklist

QuestionShelvesRelationship fileThe SAP way
Most important thing easiest to see?Partly: names and NDA badges stand out; numbers are smallYes for one party: its facts and what waits on you leadYes: the NDA and Waiting on you columns read down the list
Related things grouped?Yes: people on their company's shelfYes: everything about one relationship in one fileYes: one row per party, the details in the panel
Each label says what is behind it?YesYes: "All 3 on Contracts" says where it goesYes
Always know where you are, and the way back?Same for all three: "‹ The counter" in the head card, and Parties and People stay open as small tabs on the desk. Open party lands on Contracts narrowed to that party, as today.

What the checks changed

One door. Every option keeps "Open party" as the one way to a party's contracts. The Relationship file shows only the party's few agreements and ends in that same door.

The cheapest channel. Jonas being away is a small pill on his name and a line in his details, not a notice across the page.

Jambo guides through the rule. A deal behind a code name (Project Falcon) appears only by its code name. Its panel says why, who can add you, and offers "Ask an admin". It never shows the party.

Copilot's place. Nothing on these pages spends AI. Jambo's note in the Relationship file is built from the record, and says so.

Fit. At 1536 × 730 every option scrolls inside the desk: the lists are longer than one screen, as they are today. The head card and the panel stay put.

Questions for you

A deal behind a code name, on Parties. I drew Project Falcon as a row showing only its code name, number, stream and stage, the same stand-in Contracts shows to people outside its list. The other choice is to leave it off Parties entirely. Which do you want?
