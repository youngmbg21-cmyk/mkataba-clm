HaTi without grey

Decided: Ink Wash colours and headers in sentence case instead of capitals, on every page and every pop-up. Text sizes stay as they are today. This page shows the result on five HaTi screens next to how they look today, and sets out the plan. Nothing in HaTi has been changed yet.

Ink WashEvery grey in light mode becomes one ink: black mixed with the theme colour, softened with a pale wash of Green or Blue.
Pop-ups tooDialogs, side drawers, dropdown lists and menus, small "Saved" messages and hover hints all follow the same two changes.
Sentence case"ESSENTIALS" becomes "Essentials", a little bolder so it holds its place at today's size. About 260 places in HaTi set labels in capitals today.
Not touchedDark mode stays exactly as it is today. The contract paper keeps its colours and its own text size. The redline cards' buttons and red danger buttons keep their colours. Every text size stays the same.

The quiet colours in this view

Each number is how strongly the colour stands out from the card behind it. 4.5 or more is the accessibility pass mark for normal text; placeholders and greyed-out buttons may sit lower. In Dark mode both views show today's colours, because dark mode is not changing.

The plan, for when you say go

  1. Colours. On pages and in pop-ups, redefine HaTi's light-mode grey shades as Ink Wash, once for Green and once for Blue. Replace the ~70 stray grey codes with those shades. Grey status pills (Draft, Under review) become a pale theme tint. The redline cards' buttons are left out of this on purpose.
  2. Sentence case. Switch off capitals in the one shared label rule, then the remaining ~260 places one by one, so every label reads like "Essentials", one step bolder than today. This includes pop-up titles and dropdown group names.
  3. Check every place it shows. Desktop and phone, the other side's page, Home's board and charts, every kind of pop-up, both themes, light and dark. Then run the tests. Some tests deliberately pin today's colours and capital letters, so those get updated as part of this change.

One thing to agree before building

  • Emails, PDF exports and printed reports carry their own fixed colours. I suggest leaving them as they are in this first pass.

What does not change

  • Every text size.
  • Dark mode, exactly as today.
  • The contract paper: the sheet, every word, clause numbers, the footer and the red and green redline marks.
  • The signing copy, PDF and Word files.
  • The buttons on the redline cards: Accept, Reject, Counter, Edit, Send, Discard, and "Send all". They keep exactly today's colours. Open Negotiate to check.
  • Red danger buttons such as Delete and Discard, in pages and pop-ups.
  • Contract wording shown inside a pop-up.
  • Amber, green and red status colours.
  • Contract references such as HT-2026-0142 stay in their code style.
  • No new banners or notices.
