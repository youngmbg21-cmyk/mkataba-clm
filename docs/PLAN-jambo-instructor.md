# Jambo the instructor — the plan, end to end (owner, 23 Nov 2026)

> *"I want Jambo to help guide a new user into how to use Hati if someone was
> dropped into Hati without any training."* · *"Firstly, someone should simply
> say I am new and that how jambo knows."* · *"Both, build it now. But I need
> an end to end plan not just building the welcome plan."* — the owner, 22–23 Nov 2026.

Read this before touching what Jambo says to a newcomer, the "I'm new" reader
(js/teach.js), or the Handbook's role as the thing he teaches from. The Six
Questions, the Nine Flow Rules, NO NEW BANDS and the one-page Home plan still
apply to every round below.

---

## 1. The goal, in one sentence

A person dropped into HaTi with no training says "I'm new", and Jambo walks
them from their first sign-in to doing their real work alone — then steps
back when they say they know their way.

## 2. The rules every round keeps

1. **The person says so; Jambo never guesses.** "I'm new" turns teaching on;
   "I know my way now" turns it off. Kept on the person's own preferences, seen
   by nobody else, an admin included.
2. **One book.** Everything he teaches comes from the Handbook, the one book
   checked against the app. No second manual, no lesson text that can drift
   from the chapters.
3. **Free.** Teaching never spends an AI call on its own. A person's own
   question typed to him still goes to the brain as today.
4. **In his panel only.** Teaching is turns in the one conversation and chips
   under them. No band, banner, strip or pop-up (NO NEW BANDS). The only other
   place is the grey example words in his empty box.
5. **He shows, you press.** He moves you to where a step happens through the
   doors that already exist; he never presses, sends, signs or files.
6. **Fits what you may do.** A Viewer is taught reading; an editor uploading,
   drafting and agreeing; an admin also the team and the rules.
7. **Both languages, both screens.** Every word in English and Swedish; works
   in every Jambo box, the phone's included.

## 3. Where HaTi stands today (the review, 22 Nov 2026)

What already works:
- The Handbook: 56 chapters covering the whole app, with numbered steps.
- Jambo answers "about HaTi" questions from the book, with the chapter as a
  button; "How do I …?" opens the chapter even without an AI key.
- He knows the page you are on and what you may do there.
- Typing a page's name moves you there.

What was missing:
1. He never started the conversation for a newcomer.
2. He did not know the person was new.
3. He told, but did not walk you through step by step.
4. He cannot point at a button (the house rule: a page you land on shows no light).
5. There is nothing safe to practise on (a new company starts with an empty book).
6. Most real answers need the AI key.
7. The Handbook's pictures may be older than this week's page changes.

## 4. The rounds

Each round is its own piece of work, built only on the owner's yes, with its
tests and its Handbook lines in the same change.

### Round 1 — "I'm new" — BUILT 23 Nov 2026

- Typed in any Jambo box, "I'm new" (also "teach me", "show me around", "where
  do I start", "jag är ny", "visa mig runt") turns teaching on. "I know my way
  now" ("jag hittar nu") turns it off. The whole sentence is read: "I'm new to
  this NDA with Volvo" is a contract question, not a request to be taught.
- Jambo answers at once, free: a hello, what HaTi is for in one sentence, and
  three or four first steps as buttons, each opening its Handbook chapter. The
  steps fit the person: an editor gets Upload a contract · Start a new
  agreement · Find a contract · How the Board works; a Viewer gets Find · Read
  a contract · the Board; an admin also gets Add your team.
- The answer is kept on the person's own preferences (`teach`: on · off), so it
  follows them to every computer; nobody else sees it, an admin included.
- For a person's first 14 days, until they say either, the grey words in
  Jambo's empty box read: New here? Say "I'm new".
- The welcome email an admin's invitation sends ends with: New to HaTi? Once
  you are in, tell Jambo "I'm new" and he will show you around.
- Handbook: the Ask Jambo chapter says how. Test: f1182.

### Round 2 — "Walk me through it"

What the person sees: under each first step, and under any how-to answer while
teaching is on, a button **Walk me through it**. Jambo then gives ONE step at a
time in his panel: "Step 2 of 5 — press Upload on the Board." He takes you to
where that step happens. When HaTi sees the step is done (the page opened, the
file read, the contract created), he moves on by himself. **Back**, **Skip**
and **Stop** at any time. At the end: "Done — that's how you upload. What next?"

How it is built:
- The steps are the chapter's own numbered "how" steps — no new text.
- Each step gains a small "done when" note in the chapter (a page is showing,
  a thing exists): written once, checked by a test so a step whose check can no
  longer be met fails.
- The place each step happens is already known: the Handbook's picture recipes
  name the exact thing on the screen for each numbered step.

Decision for the owner: **may Jambo gently mark the button a step is about?**
Today's rule is "a page you land on shows no light". A soft outline only while
walking someone through, gone on the press, would be the one exception. If
not, he names the button by its words and its place ("top right, Upload").

### Round 3 — "What can I do here?" on every page

While teaching is on, the row of doors above Jambo's box (which already exists
on every page) starts with **What can I do here?**. Pressed, he answers free
from the Handbook chapters that cover the page and the flow map: what the page
is for, the two or three things people do here, and where work goes next. After
a person's third visit to a page it drops out of that page's row.

### Round 4 — Your first steps, ticked as you go

While teaching is on, Jambo's Overview on the Board shows a short list of first
tasks for the person's role (for an editor: upload a contract · find it again ·
fill in its details · send it for review · see what you owe). Each ticks itself
from what HaTi already records (the trail of what the person did) — never asked,
never typed. When all are ticked, Jambo asks once: "You know your way — shall I
stop the lessons?"

Decision for the owner: this list sits inside Jambo's panel (no band), but it
is a new part of the panel. Yes or no.

### Round 5 — A practice contract

A sample agreement only the new person can see, clearly marked **Practice**:
it can be uploaded, redlined, filled in and "sent" to a pretend other side, but
it can never be sent to a real address, signed for real, counted on the Board,
in reports, or seen by anyone else. Deleted in one press, and removed by itself
when teaching is turned off.

Decision for the owner: today a new company starts with an empty book on
purpose. Is a private practice contract allowed?

### Round 6 — Plain words for HaTi's own words

New people trip on HaTi's names: Dossier, Redlines, Playbook, stream, Board.
While teaching is on, the first time one of these appears in Jambo's answer it
carries a one-line meaning, and "what is a stream?" is answered free from a
short word list in the Handbook (a new "HaTi's words" page, both languages).
Grey buttons already say why they are grey; while teaching is on, Jambo adds
who can lift it and the proper step (his existing "Ask an admin" door).

### Round 7 — Keep the book true

A teacher with an old book misleads. Before rounds 2–4 go live: retake every
Handbook picture (only when the owner asks — this plan asks), re-read the
chapters for the pages redesigned in the week of 20 Nov (side panels, the
Stripe-way pages, Settings), and add the first-steps chapters to the browser
check that already proves each picture's boxes still find their targets.

### Round 8 — The admin's side

- Settings › People never shows who said "I'm new" (it is the person's own).
- A company switch, admins only: "Show new people how to ask Jambo for help"
  (the 14-day hint). On by default.
- The invitation and Resend welcome carry the "I'm new" line (built in round 1).

## 5. How we know it works

- **The newcomer exam**: a list of 40 things a new person types on day one
  ("how do I start", "what is a dossier", "where are my contracts", "can I
  sign this"), in both languages, each with the chapter or door it must reach —
  measured like the typing exam, with a floor that only rises.
- One browser check per round on GitHub, red on main before the round, green after.
- Photos at the owner's three screens before every round merges.
