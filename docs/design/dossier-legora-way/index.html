The Dossier, the Legora Way

One concept for the panel beside the paper, in Legora's grammar and HaTi's ink. Legora's screens are a conversation that leaves artefacts behind: the ask as a grey pill, files as chips, a workflow card that says "4 steps · Completed", a tabular review whose columns carry an icon and whose rows cite the document, then the words. The Dossier already holds these readings; this draws them that way. The frame is the laptop at 1440×780. Three moments in a contract's life, then the grammar sheet.
