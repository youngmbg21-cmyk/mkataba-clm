2 · Buttons

Height comes from a rung (--ctl-h), padding is horizontal only — padding:6px 12px on a 14px/1.2 label computes to 30.8px and quietly invents a half-rung box. One filled act per row; the rest are outlined or plain, and none of them is grey, which this product has learned three times reads as furniture.

3 · Status & semantics

Five tonal roles, four tokens each (-bg / -fg / -dot / -line), every one with a dark answer. Colour is never the only carrier — the word travels with the tone. Use the chip where a shape helps a scan; use the quiet form inside a table row, where five filled chips down the middle read as five buttons.

5 · Drawer, modal, toast

A drawer is for a form you fill and dismiss; a modal is for a question you answer. Both trap focus, both close on Escape and on the scrim, and both return focus to whatever opened them. A refusal lives in the foot — above the fields it pushes everything down 46px the moment it appears and cannot be scrolled back to.
