What you are looking at. Eight schemes on eight real HaTi screens, in
    light and dark. The screens carry the product's own ladders — 2px corners on the
    platform and square corners on the contract paper, IBM Plex Sans, the two reading
    inks, the 44px shell bar over the 240px column.

    
How a scheme is built. Each one sets the ten-step accent ramp, the
    navigation's own deeper ramp, the page ground and the two inks. Nothing else. That is
    why a new scheme is one block of CSS and not a redesign.

    
Measured, not asserted. Every scheme here clears WCAG AA on all eleven
    pairs the product depends on. Worth knowing: the teal running today is the
    weakest of the eight on its primary fill (5.47:1 white-on-accent); every new
    scheme sits between 6.70 and 11.30.
