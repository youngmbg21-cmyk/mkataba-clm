What is wrong with the page today

  • Every label is a machine name in capitals ("Landlord Principal Place Of Business Address") where a person would say "Landlord's address".
  • The right column shouts the same heading on every row and never shows the sentence the blank sits in, so it tells you nothing.
  • The boxes are different kinds and heights, so the rows do not line up, and there is a wide empty gap between each name and its box.
  • Seventeen rows run in one flat list with nothing to break them up, so you cannot see how far you are.

My recommendation: The Legora Way

It is what you picked for this page on 12 November, and the idea is still right. What was built fell short of it: the names were left as machine names, and the sentence from the paper never arrived, only a heading in capitals. Drawn properly, each row tells you exactly what you are filling in, which the paper alone does not do in one place.

If you would rather finish 17 details fast than read 17 sentences, pick The SAP Way.

The contract sentences in these drawings are examples I wrote to show the idea. I have not yet checked that HaTi can lift the real sentence around every blank on every contract. That is the first thing I would check before building The Legora Way or One at a time, and I would tell you the result before going further.
