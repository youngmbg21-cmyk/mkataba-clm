Home is a sheet of type. Your name, Jambo's three sentences, then one list where every row quotes the words it rests on.

No tiles, no cards, no big box in the middle. The verb at the end of each row is the only thing to press. Jambo's panel on the right is his conversation; his box stays at its foot.

What is new since the last round

  • Jambo's answers to the other side's changes appear as a row of their own, with their two quotes, so a negotiation waits on Home like everything else.
  • The panel says what he prepared overnight and offers the press, with its cost (free, or 1 call).
  • The kept question across your book sits in the foot, with its lights and what one more read costs.

Asks the owner first

Jambo's sentences on Home. They are the panel's Overview sentence moved onto the page. Not a band, but your rule says ask before a page says something new.

The paper with its margin. Every note stands beside the line it was read from, in the colour of what it is: amber for a blank, brown for a risk, black for something that waits on you.

The notes lay themselves out after the fonts load, so they never overlap, whatever type is on screen. That is what went wrong in your screenshot.

What is new since the last round

  • The approval is a note on the value line it rests on (the rule “over KES 20M”), with Approve and Ask Wanjiru right there.
  • The margin's colours: amber blanks, brown risks, black asks, grey facts.
  • The Dossier is a list of facts with their sources; the spine is one line of words.

Cost

On the iPad (1366 wide) the paper narrows to about 560 px, or the margin folds into the panel as the list you see on the right.

The other side's changes are marks on the paper, numbered; each number has its note in the margin and its row in the panel, with Jambo's answer already prepared from your standard and your precedents.

Nothing is sent by typing. The one filled button in the paper's line is Send all, and it says how many wait.

What is on it

  • Struck red is what they want gone, green what they want in; the dot after each ask is its number.
  • Each row in the panel: the ask in their words, Jambo's grey answer with its reasons (your standard, their last agreement with you, your precedents), then Accept · Reject · Counter, the prepared one marked.
  • Jambo says where to give and where to hold, in one sentence, and what changing a suggestion costs.

What it keeps from today

The rows' verbs, Send all with its count, the ladder, the notes and Their view are today's doors. Only the drawing changes.

Questions as columns. Ask one question of every contract once, and the answer stands in the list with its § and quote, kept, never spent again.

This is the one thing Harvey and Legora are known for, drawn HaTi's way: a quiet list, a light before each answer, the quote under it, and the cost of reading the rest said before you press.

What is on it

  • Two kept questions as columns: a yes/no one with its quote, and a number read from each payment clause.
  • “Unusual for you” is counted from the column, no call: one contract pays in 90 days where the others say 14 to 60.
  • The panel shows the question's bar (yes · no · not read), the one answer that stands out with its quote, and one button sized to what is left, with its cost.

Cost

Each question is one call per contract the first time; the list says it before the press. A changed question drops its answers.

My recommendation

The way: The Legora Way, as drawn here. It is the direction you leaned to, and these four screens show it holds across the whole day: what waits, a paper, a negotiation, and a question across the book.

The type: Editorial. Your product is about paper, and Newsreader makes the paper look like one; Inter beside it keeps every list and label readable at the small sizes HaTi uses. It is the pairing where the paper is the hero and the app steps back, which is exactly the Legora Way's idea. If you want HaTi to stay more its own, keep Today: it is a good pairing and nothing is wrong with it.

To pick, answer with two names: the way, and the type. Only those get built, and they come back to you drawn-against-built at your three screens before anything merges.
