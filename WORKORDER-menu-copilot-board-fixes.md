## W-4 · "Digging deeper · step 16 of up to 15"
**Cause:** the counter shows the step in progress (done + 1). Once all 15 steps are done and HaTi is writing the answer, it shows 16.
**Fix:** the counter never goes past its maximum. Once the steps are used up, the pill says what is happening instead ("Writing the answer"). The same check applies to the board's Dig deeper (5 steps) and the story's Dig deeper (15).

## W-5 · Board chart cards waste their space
Seen on "One owner drove recent signings" and "Payment terms is a standards weak spot":
- The ring stays a fixed size while half the card stays empty.
- The legend is squeezed into a thin column and cut off ("Young…", "SEK 6k…").

**Fix:** the chart and its legend fill the card. The legend shows its full labels, beside the ring when there is room and under it when there is not. No scroll strip when everything fits. The ring grows with the card, within a sensible limit.
