Equipment Lease & Maintenance — Juno Limited
Between Young and Juno Limited

1. The equipment

The Lessor leases to the Lessee the equipment described in Schedule 1, in good working order, for the term set out below. Delivery shall be made to Fort Knox, Indiana on or before 1 September 2026.

2. Term

This agreement begins on 13 August 2026 and continues until 12 August 2027, unless ended earlier under clause 7.

3. Rent and payment

The Lessee shall pay SEK 1,850,000 per year, payable monthly in advance. Invoices fall due 30 days from the date of issue.

4. Maintenance

The Lessor shall maintain the equipment in working order and attend to any reported fault within five (5) business days of notice.
