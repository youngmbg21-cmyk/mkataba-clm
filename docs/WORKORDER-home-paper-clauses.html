Clauses on Home's Paper

Your picks, drawn before any code is written. The clause list moves into the panel's Document tab. On Board, Paper and Explorer the white greeting row runs end to end under the blue bar, and the white panel starts below it. The panel uses the six symbols below, and the Signing tab is gone.

Home, three faces

Keys 1–3 or ← → to switch

The panel's symbols

Six tabs: Copilot · Overview · Document · Obligations · History · Deal. Signing is removed. On Board and Explorer only Copilot shows, because no contract is open. These are the same symbols as in the first version of this page: ✦ ▦ ▢ ⚑ ◷ ⇄.

The panel, what changes

  • The white greeting row goes end to end: under the blue bar, across the full width, on Board, Paper and Explorer.
  • The panel starts below that row and runs to the bottom of the screen. On Paper its symbol row lines up exactly with the contract line (same height, one shared bottom line).
  • White: the panel is plain white from top to bottom, with no grey frame around it.
  • Document tab = the clauses: the same list as the contract's own Document tab. The 8 blanks, the Copies buttons and the "left edge" line are removed.
  • Signing tab removed: its symbol and its number (2) are gone from the panel.
  • The 8 on the Document symbol counts the "8 to review" in the list below it.
