# /brag plan — HaTi

## 9-question rubric
1. **What is the app?** HaTi is a Contract Lifecycle Management (CLM) platform for the Kenyan market: draft, negotiate, sign and track contracts end to end, with an AI copilot that reads every contract.
2. **Most impressive/specific claim?** An AI copilot reads the whole portfolio and flags what needs you (Kenya-specific risks: foreign governing law, auto-renewal, stamp duty); signatures are sealed with SHA-256 over the exact frozen text.
3. **Visual hook?** HaTi's signature split: "the product" (teal shell, mono data) vs "the paper" (cream contract page with tracked redlines). The cream page with a live redline is the money shot.
4. **What to show from the real UI?** Cream contract paper + tracked redline (teal insertion / red strike), a Copilot brief card with risk flags, an Accept/Counter negotiation card, the Portfolio-Intelligence contract graph, and the SHA-256 signature seal.
5. **Shortest satisfying video?** ~21 seconds. Six tight beats.
6. **Tone?** Preset: `polished`. Direction: quiet, premium enterprise product film — confident, restrained, product-forward. (HaTi's own design ethos is SAP-S/4HANA restraint; no gimmicks.)
7. **Audio?** One warm upbeat-corporate music bed (bundled "Business Moves"), gentle fade-out under the outro. Two restrained SFX: a soft UI click on redline Accept, a low seal impact when the signature locks. No voiceover.
8. **Format?** Landscape 1920×1080, 30fps.
9. **Colour identity (HaTi teal brand):** deep teal ground #093733/#0B4A45, accent #0E5F58 / #2E8C82 / #5EC2B3, pale mint #E1F0ED/#F0F7F5, ink #1B2A28/#5F6D6B, cream paper #FFFDF9. Product face IBM Plex Sans; data in mono; the paper set in a serif so "paper" reads instantly.

## Creative angle
The contract lifecycle, handled — shown as one continuous flow: paper → copilot reads it → you negotiate → the graph shows the whole book → it's signed and sealed. Every scene shows a real HaTi surface and uses HaTi's own words.

## Storyboard (≈21.0s, 30fps)
- **S1 Hook (0.0–3.4)** — Cream contract paper rises on the teal ground. A clause redlines live (red strike on old text, teal-underlined insertion). Line: "Every contract. Every clause. Handled."
- **S2 Copilot reads (3.4–7.2)** — Teal shell. A Copilot brief card writes in; three Kenya risk flags tick on (Foreign governing law · Auto-renewal · Stamp duty). Line: "Copilot read all 30. Here's what needs you."
- **S3 Negotiate (7.2–11.0)** — A redline change card: insertion + deletion, then an Accept press (soft click, card turns teal "Accepted · Round 2"). Line: "Redline, counter, agree — every round on the record."
- **S4 Portfolio graph (11.0–14.8)** — Contract nodes scatter then cluster into value-stream hubs; a filter line resolves. Line: "See the whole book. Ask it anything."
- **S5 Sign & seal (14.8–18.4)** — A signature line completes; a SHA-256 seal chip drops in and locks (low impact). Line: "Signed. Sealed with SHA-256."
- **S6 Outro (18.4–21.0)** — Wordmark **HaTi** with teal underline; tagline "Contract Lifecycle Management" and closer "The whole lifecycle, in one place." Music fades.

### Music cue guidance
Track: assets/music/bed.mp3 ("Happy Beats / Business Moves vol.10"). Bundled cue preset available. Treat cues as optional timing hints; keep readability and product clarity primary. Land the S5 seal and the S6 wordmark on strong beats where convenient; fade music out over the last ~1.4s.
