WAREHOUSING AND TRANSPORTATION SERVICES AGREEMENT

Contract · Jurisdiction: Sweden (EU/GDPR)

This Warehousing and Transportation Services Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into between: (“Customer”), and (“AIT” or “Service Provider”).

The parties agree as follows:

1. Services and Applicable Terms

AIT shall provide warehousing, transportation and related logistics services to the Customer in accordance with this Agreement. Agreement only.
The services shall be governed by: The Nordic Association of Freight Forwarders’ General Conditions (NSAB 2015).
AIT’s standard terms and conditions, if any, shall be attached as Appendix and must be signed by both parties to form part of this Agreement.

2. Term and Termination

This Agreement shall remain in force for an initial term of three (3) years from the Effective Date.

The parties shall review renewal options at least three (3) months before the expiry of the initial term.

Either party may terminate this Agreement by providing six (6) months’ written notice.

Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if:

a) the other party becomes insolvent, enters bankruptcy proceedings, or makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors; or

b) the Customer’s goods are materially affected by a casualty event, force majeure incident, or condemnation affecting the warehouse where the goods are stored.

3. Pricing and Payment

The Customer shall pay all rates and charges specified in the applicable rate schedules and appendices to this Agreement.

Where no agreed rate exists, AIT shall provide a written quotation before performing the requested service.

AIT may adjust pricing if there are significant increases in external costs, including fuel, taxes, duties, tolls, levies, or where information provided by the Customer materially affects service costs.

4. Liability

AIT’s liability for loss of or damage to goods shall be limited in accordance with the applicable mandatory transport conventions.
